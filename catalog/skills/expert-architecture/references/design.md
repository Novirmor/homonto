# Architecture proposal outline

- Goal, scope and decision to make
- Evidence: current components, constraints and accepted decisions
- Assumptions and questions that could change the recommendation
- Alternatives, trade-offs and recommended approach
- Boundaries, ownership, interfaces and data flow
- Relevant diagrams with their source and rendering status
- Failure behavior, consistency and recovery
- Deployment, observability and operational ownership
- Incremental implementation/migration plan and acceptance criteria
- Proposed decisions and remaining validation

For a focused answer, use only the sections that clarify the decision. An
implementation handoff should identify the exact interfaces and invariants a
writer must preserve, plus the evidence a reviewer will need.
