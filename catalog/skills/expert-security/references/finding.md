# Security finding outline

- Title and classification: confirmed, needs validation, rejected or hardening
- Affected scope and exact evidence (paths/lines, supplied traces or observations)
- Actor, prerequisites, existing controls and reachable attack path
- Impact, confidence and severity rationale
- Smallest practical remediation
- Validation performed, missing evidence and regression checks
- Relevant verified references

For an independent challenge, give a separate reviewer the evidence and ask
whether the preconditions, reachability and impact actually follow. Record
disagreements and resolve them with evidence rather than severity labels alone.
